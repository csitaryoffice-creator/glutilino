This Notice explains how Plan Liberte Tanácsadó és Szolgáltató Limited Liability Company, the operator of the glutiliNO Artisan Gluten-Free Pizzeria and Bakery, processes the personal data of visitors to glutilino.hu.

The website is primarily for information. It does not contain a contact form, registration interface, newsletter subscription, online ordering or table-reservation system. Visitors do not need to provide personal data to use the website.

Certain technical data are nevertheless processed automatically when the website operates. Subject to the visitor’s consent, data relating to analytics and external services may also be processed.

1. What principles do we follow when processing data?

We follow these principles when processing data:

  • We process personal data lawfully, fairly and transparently.
  • We collect personal data only for specified, explicit and legitimate purposes.
  • The personal data we process are adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary for the purposes of processing.
  • We take all reasonable steps to ensure that the data we process are accurate and, where necessary, kept up to date.
  • We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to achieve the purposes of processing.
  • We protect personal data through appropriate technical and organisational measures.
  • We process personal data on a lawful basis under the GDPR and only to the extent necessary for the relevant purpose.

3. What data do we process and for what purposes?

The website has no contact or registration function through which visitors can submit personal data directly.

When the website is used, technical data and, with the visitor’s consent, analytics and external-service data may be processed.

3.1. Technical data processed when operating the website

When the website is opened, the web server may automatically log certain technical information.

Data processed: IP address, time of visit, browser type, technical information about the device and operating system, pages visited, server responses and error messages.

Purpose of processing: to ensure proper website operation, identify technical errors, maintain system security and prevent unauthorised access.

Legal basis: the controller’s legitimate interest in securely and reliably operating the website under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Retention period: technical log data are processed for the period necessary to operate the website securely, in accordance with the hosting provider’s log-retention settings.

3.2. Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand website traffic and usage.

Data processed: technical and statistical data and online identifiers relating to visits, pages viewed, browsers, devices and activity on the website.

Purpose of processing: measuring website traffic, analysing usage patterns and improving the website.

Legal basis: the visitor’s prior consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

Retention period: analytics data are retained according to the retention settings configured in Google Analytics. Visitors may withdraw consent at any time.

Google Analytics is activated only after the visitor has given valid consent.

3.3. Use of Google Maps

The website uses Google Maps to show the restaurant’s location and make navigation easier.

When the map is displayed, Google may process technical data such as IP address, browser information and map-usage data.

Purpose of processing: displaying the restaurant’s geographical location and assisting visitors with navigation.

Legal basis: the visitor’s prior consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

Google Maps content requiring consent is loaded only after the visitor has given valid consent.

Detailed information about Google’s data processing is available in Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. What are cookies and how do we use them?

Cookies are small data files that a browser may store while the website is being used.

Some cookies are necessary for website operation; others serve analytics or external-service purposes.

Strictly necessary cookies do not require prior consent. We request prior consent for all non-essential cookies.

Cookies used

Necessary cookies

Technical cookies required by the website support secure operation and remember the visitor’s cookie choices.

These cookies are not used for marketing or advertising tracking.

Analytics cookies

We use Google Analytics to measure traffic and analyse website usage.

Google Analytics may use analytics cookies such as _ga and _ga_XXXXXXXXXX.

These cookies help distinguish visits and provide statistical information about website usage.

Analytics cookies are activated only with the visitor’s prior consent.

Cookies related to external services

The website includes Google Maps, which loads only after the visitor has consented.

Google may use cookies or other technical identifiers when Google Maps operates.

Google’s applicable privacy terms also govern the processing of such data.

Marketing cookies

We currently do not use a separate advertising-tracking system such as Meta Pixel.

If such a service is introduced in the future, this Notice will be updated and the necessary consent management will be provided.

Managing cookies

The website’s cookie manager lets visitors accept or reject non-essential cookies.

Visitors may change or withdraw consent at any time in the cookie settings available on the website.

The cookie manager remembers the visitor’s choice for 12 months. Consent may still be changed or withdrawn at any time.

The 12-month period applies to retaining the cookie choice, not to the storage period of every cookie or analytics record.

Visitors may also delete or restrict cookies stored in their browser. Blocking some cookies may affect certain website functions.

7. How do we protect data?

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data when operating the website.

These measures aim to ensure that data are accessible only to authorised persons and to prevent unauthorised disclosure, alteration or loss.

Security measures may include:

  • Using a secure, encrypted HTTPS connection.
  • Restricting access to hosting and administration interfaces.
  • Password-protected access.
  • Keeping the website’s technical environment up to date.
  • Backups and appropriate access-control management.

Complete security of data transmission over the internet cannot be guaranteed, but we take every reasonable measure to reduce data-security risks.

You may exercise the following rights in relation to data processing:
