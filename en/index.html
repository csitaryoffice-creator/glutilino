Gluten-free pizza dough, slow-fermented

Great pizza takes time, experience and carefully selected ingredients. We give equal attention to all three.

  • We give the dough time

    Our gluten-free pizza dough is slowly fermented. The centre stays thin while the cornicione turns crisp and beautifully blistered — just as a proper Neapolitan pizza should.

  • Know-how from Naples

    Alex, co-owner and chef of glutiliNO, spent nearly eight years in Naples, mastering the traditions and techniques of Italian pizza-making. Today he brings that experience to glutiliNO’s gluten-free pizzas.

  • Our own recipe, Italian ingredients

    Our gluten-free pizza dough is made to Alex’s own recipe, and many of the ingredients for our toppings come directly from Italy.

The glutiliNO story: from Naples to Budapest

Alex’s professional journey and Kinga’s personal story came together around one table.

Alex came from the world of Neapolitan pizza-making. Kinga’s personal connection to gluten-free dining showed why no one should have to miss out on the pleasure of great food.

That shared idea became glutiliNO: a pizzeria and bakery where Italian flavours and a gluten-free approach naturally belong together.

Why glutiliNO?

A shared experience

You would never guess our pizzas are gluten-free. The whole family can enjoy the same Neapolitan pizza experience, whether or not they need to avoid gluten. No one has to compromise.

Championship recipe

Our pizzas are made under the professional guidance of our World and European Champion pizzaiolo, using his unique technique. This is not mass production — it is artisan craft.

Premium ingredients

We work exclusively with carefully selected gluten-free flour blends from trusted sources and first-class toppings. There is no “cheaper alternative” here — only the best.

100% safety

Our food is prepared in an environment free from cross-contamination, following strict procedures. Every stage of the process is designed so our guests can genuinely feel safe with us. For us, this is not an extra — it is fundamental.

More to discover

Tea since 1836

With roots in Hamburg, Tea since 1836 draws on almost 200 years of tea-trading tradition. Its range includes both classic teas and distinctive blends. At glutiliNO you can choose from 20 premium Tea since 1836 varieties.

Pödör seasonings

Pödör’s premium spices and seasoning blends are made from carefully selected ingredients. Alongside familiar favourites, the brand also offers more distinctive flavours. A wide selection is available at glutiliNO.
